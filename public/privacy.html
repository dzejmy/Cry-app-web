Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

SkiDesk ("we", "us", or "our") operates the SkiDesk platform for ski school management. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our service.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account data: name, email address, and password when you register.
  • School data: information about your ski school, instructors, and bookings that you enter into the platform.
  • Customer data: names, emails, and booking details of your clients, which you provide to us as a data controller.
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, and error logs collected automatically.
  • Payment data: billing information processed securely through Stripe. We do not store card numbers.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide and maintain the SkiDesk platform.
  • Send transactional emails (booking confirmations, payout summaries).
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Improve our product based on aggregated usage analytics.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with trusted sub-processors necessary to operate the service (e.g. Stripe for payments, AWS for hosting). All sub-processors are bound by data processing agreements compliant with GDPR.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. After account deletion, data is permanently removed within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it longer.

5. Your Rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@skidesk.app.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and functional cookies to remember your preferences. See our Cookie Policy for details.

7. Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest. We perform regular security audits and follow industry best practices for access control.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you by email of any material changes at least 14 days before they take effect.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at privacy@skidesk.app or use our contact form.
