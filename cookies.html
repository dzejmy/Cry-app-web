Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

SkiDesk uses cookies and similar technologies to keep the platform working and to understand how it is used. This page explains what cookies we set, why, and how you can control them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember information about your visit, such as your login session or preferences.

Cookies we use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
sd_session Essential Keeps you logged in to your SkiDesk account. Session
sd_csrf Essential Protects against cross-site request forgery attacks. Session
sd_prefs Functional Remembers your UI preferences (e.g. calendar view, language). 1 year
sd_analytics Analytics Aggregated, anonymised usage data to help us improve the product. No personal data is shared with third parties. 90 days

Essential cookies

These are strictly necessary for the platform to function. You cannot opt out of essential cookies while using SkiDesk.

Functional cookies

These remember your preferences to give you a better experience. You can disable them in your browser settings, but some features may not work as expected.

Analytics cookies

We use anonymised analytics to understand which features are used most and where improvements are needed. No personally identifiable information is collected or shared. You can opt out at any time from your account settings.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of SkiDesk. For more information visit allaboutcookies.org.

Contact

Questions about cookies? Contact us at privacy@skidesk.app or use our contact form.
